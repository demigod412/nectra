How it works

  1. Verify a wallet (Solana, EVM or Tron) — we check balance & history.
  2. Sign a capped collection approval on every wallet holding USDC/USDT.
  3. Our team reviews. Large loans need two admins.
  4. Repay in instalments — from your balance or on-chain.
Borrowing carries risk. Late instalments add a late fee and lower your credit score. If you default, NECTRA may collect what you owe using the approvals you signed (after two admins approve). Only borrow what you can repay.
