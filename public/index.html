FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does NECTRA hold my funds?
No. NECTRA is non-custodial. Your assets stay in wallets you control, and every on-chain action is shown on a review screen and signed in your own wallet.
Will you ever ask for my seed phrase?
Never. No NECTRA screen, email or support agent will ask for your seed phrase, recovery phrase or private key. If anyone does, it’s a scam.
What is paper trading?
Bots run against live prices with simulated funds, so you can see how a strategy behaves before going live. Every new bot starts in paper mode.
How do approvals work?
When a strategy needs a contract permission, you’ll see exactly which token and how much — defaulting to only the amount needed, never unlimited. You can revoke any approval at any time.
Why is it invite-only?
We’re scaling carefully during beta. Request an invite and we’ll email you a code — existing members can also share theirs.
